## Quick Steps to Add Images

### Step 1: Download Images
Download product images from:
- Manufacturer websites (Bosch, Makita, Dewalt, Black+Decker, Stanley)
- E-commerce sites (Amazon, Flipkart)
- Stock photo sites

### Step 2: Rename Files
Use lowercase with underscores:
- ✅ `jigsaw.jpg`
- ✅ `circular_saw.jpg`
- ❌ `Jigsaw.jpg` (no uppercase)
- ❌ `circular-saw.jpg` (no hyphens)

### Step 3: Copy to Project
1. Open: `app/src/main/res/drawable/`
2. Copy all image files here
3. Rebuild project

### Step 4: Update Code (After adding images)
After adding images, I can help you update the ProductListActivity.kt to use the new images. Just let me know which images you've added!

## Image Requirements
- **Format**: JPG or PNG
- **Size**: 400x400 pixels minimum (square recommended)
- **File size**: Under 500KB per image (for app performance)
- **Quality**: Clear, professional product photos
